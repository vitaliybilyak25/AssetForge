Stories must have an Epic link present before they are marked ready. The initial delivery of #13 was missing its Epic #1 link and required a correction pass after acceptance.

**Why:** Missing parent links were caught at delivery rather than at refinement, adding unnecessary rework. The ready checklist in AGILE_BOARD.md should be updated to include "Epic link present on issue" as a mandatory item.

**How to apply:** During refinement prep, flag any issue that lacks a parent Epic link as not ready. Raise the checklist gap to the PO for AGILE_BOARD.md update before Sprint 2 planning.
